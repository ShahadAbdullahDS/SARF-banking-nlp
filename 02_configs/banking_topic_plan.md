# Banking Corpus Topic Plan

## Topics
1. Money transfers and transfer status
2. Debit and credit cards
3. Account access and login
4. Banking-app experience
5. Payments and merchants
6. Cash machines and cash withdrawal

## Genre mix
- Question: about one third
- Statement: about one third
- Comment: about one third

## Length mix
- Short (5–9 words): about 35%
- Medium (10–20 words): about 50%
- Long (21–30 words): about 15%

## Pilot plan
- Banking pilot: 60 texts total.
- Use 6 topics × 10 texts each.
- Rotate genres and length buckets across topics.
- Do not use intent labels or copy ArBanking77 texts.
- Do not start full banking generation until the pilot audit is marked Pass.
