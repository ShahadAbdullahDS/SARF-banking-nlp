# General Corpus Topic Plan

## Topics
1. Food and restaurants
2. Travel and trips
3. Study and university life
4. Work and daily routines
5. Sports and fitness
6. Entertainment and hobbies
7. Transport and traffic
8. Technology and devices
9. Shopping and products (non-financial)
10. Family and social life
11. Home and daily errands
12. Weather and outdoor activities

## Genre mix
- Question: about one third
- Statement: about one third
- Comment: about one third

## Length mix
- Short (5–9 words): about 35%
- Medium (10–20 words): about 50%
- Long (21–30 words): about 15%

## Pilot plan
- General pilot: 240 texts total.
- Use 12 topics × 1 genre × 1 length bucket × 20 texts.
- Rotate genres and length buckets across topics so the pilot includes all three genres and all three length buckets.
- Banking pilot: 60 texts total, generated separately.
- Do not start full generation until the pilot audit is marked Pass.
