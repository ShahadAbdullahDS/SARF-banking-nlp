# Pilot Audit Rubric

## Review each sampled text
| Check | Pass when... |
|---|---|
| Saudi style | The wording sounds naturally Saudi-style, not formal MSA or unnatural Arabic. |
| Clarity | The text is understandable and self-contained. |
| Genre fit | The text matches its recorded genre. |
| Length fit | The text matches its recorded length bucket. |
| Privacy | The text contains no personal or sensitive information. |
| General-pool safety | General text contains no banking or financial terms. |
| Duplication | The text is not a template copy or near-duplicate of another sampled text. |

## Pilot decision rule
- Audit a random sample of at least 30 general texts and 15 banking texts.
- Mark every item as Pass, Fail, or Unsure.
- Revise the prompt if more than 10% of reviewed texts fail a required check, or if banking leakage appears in the general pool.
- Start bulk generation only after the pilot decision is recorded as Pass.
